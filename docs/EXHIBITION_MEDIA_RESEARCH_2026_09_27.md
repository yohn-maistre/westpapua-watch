# Exhibition media research · 27 September 2026

This pass reviewed the 54 existing Exhibition records and one new ARTJOG record. The public catalogue and the moving wall serve different jobs: the catalogue keeps every source-linked record; the wall selects works and documentation that can carry a visual sequence. A source-only catalogue entry is never presented as a photograph of its subject.

## Source-confirmed additions

- **Alyakha Kolektif, _Kiney Waloboney_ (2025), ARTJOG 2026.** ARTJOG's artwork page gives the medium (wood, netting, clay, sound installation, iron and plywood), collective authorship, and Sentani/Yokiwa context. The record is source-linked; its artwork images are not imported here. https://artjog.id/2026/artwork.php?id=5&lang=en&name=Alyakha-Kolektif&token=e4da3b7fbbce2345d7772b0674a318d5
- **Hidden Faces of Papua.** Framer Framed names Bekaku Ba, Tewed, Toboo, Karwar, Nemangkawi Holes, KUG and Bihm as parts of one collaborative presentation, and credits installation photography to Maarten Nauw / Framer Framed. A named part alone does not establish a separate reusable image for each part. https://framerframed.nl/en/dossier/hidden-faces-of-papua-door-danielle-hofmans/
- **Read My World 2026.** The official Opening Night programme names DEFE Wabiser and gispa ferdinanda warijo among its participants. The festival liveblog links Opening Night and subsequent photo galleries credited to Manoah Salampessy. These galleries are useful source destinations; a published gallery is not itself a reuse grant. https://readmyworld.nl/en/program-item/opening-night-2026/ and https://readmyworld.nl/
- **Sa Pu Kisah.** Its own public exhibition presents individual Papuan women's artworks. The Watch records keep artist and work as primary metadata and Sa Pu Kisah / Udeido / AJAR as exhibition provenance. Direct relationships with organizers are a useful route for future media clearance, but the website does not encode those relationships as a blanket license. https://sapukisah.asia-ajar.org/pameran-online/

## Image status

At the start of this pass, 13 of 54 records had attributed external or partner-context imagery; 41 were source-only graphic plates. The moving wall now selects the image-backed entries plus a small edit of explicitly typographic programme/archive plates. The full source-only collection remains in the searchable index.

The Met's Open Access policy covers images of **public-domain** works, not every image on an object page. Its collection API marks the Watch's linked Cenderawasih objects 318765, 313896, 316083, 313777, 311201, 311994 and 311560 as `isPublicDomain: false` with no `primaryImage`. The Met entries therefore remain source-linked plates; visible site thumbnails are not ingested as CC0 media. https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access and https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/318765

Papua Erfgoed's 1962 film record remains metadata-first and links to the archive viewer. The optional Wurm–Hattori Language Atlas polygon research is a historical map lead, outside this Exhibition media pass.
