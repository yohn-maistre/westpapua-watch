# Exhibition media research · 27 September 2026

This pass reviewed the 54 existing Exhibition records and added 11 source-confirmed records. The catalogue keeps every source-linked record; the moving wall edits the image-backed works and a few distinctly typographic reference plates. A source-only entry is never presented as a photograph of its subject.

## Source-confirmed additions

- **Alyakha Kolektif, _Kiney Waloboney_ (2025), ARTJOG 2026.** ARTJOG's artwork page gives the medium (wood, netting, clay, sound installation, iron and plywood), collective authorship, and Sentani/Yokiwa context. The record is source-linked; its artwork images are not imported here. https://artjog.id/2026/artwork.php?id=5&lang=en&name=Alyakha-Kolektif&token=e4da3b7fbbce2345d7772b0674a318d5
- **Hidden Faces of Papua.** Framer Framed names Bekaku Ba, Tewed, Toboo, Karwar, Nemangkawi Holes, KUG and Bihm as parts of one collaborative presentation, and credits installation photography to Maarten Nauw / Framer Framed. A named part alone does not establish a separate reusable image for each part. https://framerframed.nl/en/dossier/hidden-faces-of-papua-door-danielle-hofmans/
- **Read My World 2026.** The official Opening Night programme names DEFE Wabiser and gispa ferdinanda warijo among its participants. The festival liveblog links Opening Night and subsequent photo galleries credited to Manoah Salampessy. These galleries are useful source destinations; a published gallery is not itself a reuse grant. https://readmyworld.nl/en/program-item/opening-night-2026/ and https://readmyworld.nl/
- **Sa Pu Kisah.** A Humanisma exhibition article documents individual works and names the artists. The Watch now presents ten image-backed works by Betty Adii, Blandina Yeimo, Irene Wagab, Nancy Nahuway, Ritha Karubuy, Jenita Hilapok, Diana Yembise, Dessy Baru, Nadila Aibini and Aquino Renwarin. The artist and work lead each record; the exhibition is provenance. The images remain attributed previews hosted by Humanisma, with a direct link to its article. The former exhibition site currently does not reliably serve its own catalogue. https://humanisma.wordpress.com/2021/03/23/sa-pu-kisah-perempuan-papua-dalam-kepungan-lingkaran-kekerasan/
- **Mairi.** IndoArtNow documents seven individually named works, media and artist credits from the exhibition: Yanto Gombo's _Di Negeriku_; Ignasius Dicky Takndare's _Deep Silent Sio Insose_ and _The Death Dresses_; Michael Yan Devis's _Feminism For Mairi_; Andre Takimai's _Gimi Gai Matiyoo_; Costantinus Raharusun's _Mataku Prespektifmu_; and Nelson Natkime's _Nao Temea Lamenge_. These are source-linked, attributed external previews, not files ingested into the repo. https://indoartnow.com/exhibitions/pameran-seni-rupa-mairi

## Image status

At the start of this pass, 13 of 54 records had attributed external or partner-context imagery; 41 were source-only. The current 65 records include 24 image-backed works and 41 source-only references. A failed external preview resolves to a labelled typographic reference, never an unrelated abstract picture. The full collection remains searchable in the catalogue.

The Met's Open Access policy covers images of **public-domain** works, not every image on an object page. Its collection API marks the Watch's linked Cenderawasih objects 318765, 313896, 316083, 313777, 311201, 311994 and 311560 as `isPublicDomain: false` with no `primaryImage`. The Met entries therefore remain source-linked plates; visible site thumbnails are not ingested as CC0 media. https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access and https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/318765

Papua Erfgoed's 1962 film record remains metadata-first and links to the archive viewer. The optional Wurm–Hattori Language Atlas polygon research is a historical map lead, outside this Exhibition media pass.

## Next media clearance queue

Request or document reusable high-resolution stills from Sa Pu Kisah artists and the Pusaka/Udeido network, the Mairi artists and photographers, Framer Framed's installation photographers, Read My World/Manoah Salampessy, and Papua Erfgoed. Capture the file, creator, work, credit line, scope and date of permission per item. A direct working relationship helps make that possible; it is not a blanket media license. Until then, source-linked attributed previews can represent confirmed artworks, while unverified individual performer imagery stays off their records. In particular, an Opening Night gallery does not yet identify a specific reusable photograph of DEFE or gispa.
