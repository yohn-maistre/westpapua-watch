## Weekly synthesis

Home now previews the weekly review rather than repeating the Merauke case analysis. Case-specific analysis remains inside its case. The accepted review stays accessible in News with sources and edition navigation.

The existing scheduled writer/critic workflow now asks for a supported argument connecting distinct episodes and time, with Papuan agency, consequences, relevant responses/counterevidence and observable developments to watch. Shared names are not causal mechanisms; historical context is not evidence of a new event. Per-thread validation requires two publisher families and distinct episodes/context records and rejects exact introductory/prior-edition repetition. Root-domain grouping prevents regional subdomains counting as independent publishers; it is a heuristic, not proof against syndication or common ownership. The critic must inspect the cited original excerpts. Matching citations alone do not establish support.

Input size is bounded (sixteen recent articles, up to eight contextual articles and eight historical excerpts, each up to 1,200 characters); the critic receives the cited evidence subset. Routes, model configuration, budgets, schedule and database schema are unchanged. Existing published editions are not regenerated. Unsupported drafts remain held and the last accepted review stays visible. Mocked tests verify these controls, not the semantic accuracy of a future model-authored edition. Operator review and stronger-model escalation remain future options rather than shipped services.
