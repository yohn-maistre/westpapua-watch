# Source map 04

The registry treats these as provenance categories, not a numerical trust ranking. A source's ownership and role should remain visible wherever it materially affects interpretation.

## Enabled launch discovery

- **Jubi** — Papua-focused newsroom; core local reporting.
- **Aneta Papua** — Papua-focused alternative/community reporting.
- **Mongabay Indonesia** — environment, land, biodiversity and extractive-industry reporting; mixed Indonesia scope, so Papua relevance is filtered.
- **Suara Papua** — Papua-focused reporting; keep source attribution visible.
- **Project Multatuli** — investigative/long-form reporting; mixed Indonesia scope, so Papua relevance is filtered.

## Registered, disabled until adapter/feed review

- Lao-Lao Papua
- Nadi Papua
- BBC News Indonesia
- RNZ Pacific
- ABC Pacific
- RRI
- ANTARA

RRI and ANTARA are useful as attributed public/state records and local supplements, not as substitutes for independent corroboration. BBC/RNZ/ABC are useful external reporting layers when relevant to Papua or the Pacific.

## Durable-resource publishers and collections

Candidates worth building adapters or curated imports for include:

- Yayasan Pusaka Bentala Rakyat
- Human Rights Monitor
- Papuan Voices
- Kalawai and other Papua civil-society archives
- ACLED and other structured conflict-event datasets
- UN treaty, General Assembly and human-rights records
- Indonesian legislation, court decisions and official statistical publications
- peer-reviewed research on Papua/New Guinea, Melanesia, Indigenous governance, political history, public health, ecology, biodiversity, forestry, fisheries and extractive industries
- university repositories and books with durable bibliographic records
- documentary film, recorded talks, oral-history collections and cultural archives

## Resource tags

Resource candidates now support multiple tags. Prefer tags that can connect the archive without forcing every item into one hierarchy:

```text
place: raja-ampat, sentani, merauke, highlands, west-new-guinea, melanesia
issue: mining, displacement, political-status, food-estate, women-gender
field: human-rights, law, ecology, biodiversity, anthropology, political-science
form: report, research, book, dataset, film, talk, archive
concept: extractivism, ecocide, self-determination, customary-land, indigeneity
```

The engine currently derives tags from Story Packet topics, places and issue candidates. Editorial review should normalize spelling and merge aliases before a larger corpus accumulates.
