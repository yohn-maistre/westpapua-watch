## Keep across the site

- Audit the actual page and data before choosing a composition. Use before/after screenshots.
- Use the existing Instrument Sans/Serif and CSS tokens. Controls should share dimensions and states; use Base UI where focus, dismissal or keyboard selection need it.
- Separate with space, alignment and restrained surface changes. Avoid nesting bordered cards around every object or repeating eyebrow/title/subtitle explanations.
- Prefer real source imagery, documents and meaningful data to invented decoration. Preserve captions, original links and credits. Do not invent people, quotes, images of incidents or numbers to fill a composition.
- Motion gives selection and navigation feedback. No scroll hijacking, perpetual simulation or animation requirement that delays evidence on a phone.
- Keep touch targets usable, text legible and reading order coherent at 320–390px and increased text size. A desktop workspace must become a deliberate phone reading flow.
