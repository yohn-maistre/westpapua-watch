## Strong gallery clusters

### 1. Living Papuan contemporary art
Udeido individual works, Alyakha, named Papuan makers and newer Sentani practice.

### 2. Papuan-made moving image
Papuan Voices works can become a real film shelf rather than one generic tile: *Perempuan di Tanahnya*, *Mama Kasmira Pu Mau*, *Monce Truck*, *The Caretaker of Isio Hill Forest*, *Ironic Survival*, and others.

### 3. Arnold Ap / Mambesak sonic history
Mambesak archival audio, *Muman Minggil*, the Anceaux recordings and later musical work can become a dedicated listening/history cluster.

### 4. Historical moving image
*Uang Eka* and *From Stone Age to Atom Age* contain rare footage of daily life, education, money, the New Guinea Council and work. Their historical/colonial framing should be explicit.

### 5. Museum objects without turning the gallery into an object dump
Use a small number of exceptionally visual Sentani, Raja Ampat, Asmat/Auyu and Cenderawasih Bay objects, always with maker/community, catalogue provenance and institution.

### 6. Archive as visual history
Leiden photographs and the 1945 map can work as quiet interstitial records among contemporary art.
