## Saturation verdict

**The Library is ready to ship as a serious research collection.** It is not literally complete, because Papua's knowledge landscape includes print-only books, unpublished archaeological reports, church archives, district grey literature, oral knowledge never digitised, and university repositories whose metadata is only partly indexed. But the shape of the returns changed sharply in Pass 2: remaining discoveries increasingly look like specialist theses, individual archive folders, object records, micro-datasets and grey literature rather than missing canonical foundations.

That is the signal to stop delaying launch for one more giant excavation. Ingest the strong corpus now and make discovery an ongoing editorial maintenance workflow.

## What Pass 2 added that Pass 1 was missing

- **Papuan/local scholarship:** UNCEN, UNIPA, Musamus, Papuan customary-law researchers, Indigenous health researchers, and Papuan intellectuals.
- **Language as primary material:** Yali dictionary/corpus, Wano and Abawiri grammars, Yelmek documentation, Eipo oral recordings, Salawati and Ma’ya datasets, large lexicographic bibliographies.
- **Everyday public life:** TB/HIV, malaria, leprosy, reproductive health, stunting, teacher absenteeism, local budgets, migration, public expenditure and rural services.
- **Religion and Indigenous theology:** Benny Giay, Koreri/Manarmakeri studies, Papuan contextual theology and mission history.
- **Natural history:** Mamberamo forest ecology, Cyclops birds, Bird’s Head DNA barcoding, Raja Ampat reefs, Maybrat karst, orchids, fish, amphibians/reptiles and community conservation.
- **Primary archives:** Nationaal Archief UNTEA and colonial administrative files, historical maps, Leiden item-level images and archival guides.
- **Moving image and sound:** Papuan Voices, Mambesak/Arnold Ap audio, Anceaux field recordings, archival Baliem films, MIFEE documentaries, contemporary Papuan film and sonic work.

## Practical ingestion rule

1. **Ship P0 + verified first.** This gives the public Library breadth without exposing unresolved metadata.
2. **P1 verified/light-QA is wave 2.** Most are good resources that need only a quick canonical-title/creator check.
3. **Bibliographic leads stay in staging.** Do not discard them; they are valuable targets for future archive/library resolution.
4. **Keep archive hubs as resources.** A corpus, repository, archive or database is itself useful even before every child item is indexed.
5. **Index individual films and audio works.** They are discoverable objects, not merely footnotes to Papuan Voices/Mambesak hubs.

## Why the final corpus should not pretend to be complete

A mature Pustaka should make incompleteness legible. `lastVerifiedAt`, `metadataStatus`, `sourcePass`, and eventually a small editorial `needsReview` flag are enough. The collection can then expand continuously without turning every new discovery into a redesign or launch blocker.
