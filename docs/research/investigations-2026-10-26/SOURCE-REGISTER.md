# Source register

Research cut-off: 26 October 2026. Forty-three retrieved documents/pages were inspected to the scopes below; one CIA lead returned a maintenance page. Some PDFs and long reports were read only in targeted sections. Retrieval is not an endorsement or independent verification of every statement in a source. Financial figures, ownership and court status retain their original dates.

`SOURCES.json` contains machine-readable metadata and source-family identifiers. The query log records discovery, including rejected/noisy results. Reprints, coalition reports and source-dependent reporting must not be counted as multiple independent confirmations.
