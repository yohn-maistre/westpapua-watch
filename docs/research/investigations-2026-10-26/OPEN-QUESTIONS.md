# Consequential gaps and next documents

Research cut-off: 26 October 2026. These items are **not established facts**.

1. **Global Papua Abadi ownership.** This pass did not establish a reliable GPA–Djarum/Hartono/Salim ownership relationship. Search matches included irrelevant employment references, tags and social posts. Obtain dated AHU/company deeds and the concession-holder chain. Historical Salim/Indogunta research is a separate lead.
2. **Haji Isam's political finance.** Documented commercial roles and reported political closeness do not establish a donation amount, all funding channels or a quid pro quo. Acquire full campaign schedules and underlying reporting/transaction evidence where public. Read accessible Tempo reporting within its scope; don't turn a headline into a financial ledger.
3. **Machinery financing and use.** SANY's order announcement supplies quantity/counterparties, not a verified price, ultimate payer, full delivery record or location of every machine. Distinguish different reported prices and batches until original order/finance documents clarify them.
4. **Rice, sugar/bioethanol and road authority.** Keep each project's operator, procurement, land instrument and budget separate. Common political sponsorship does not merge them into one unchanged programme.
5. **October 2026 forest policy announcement.** Which decrees, permits, budgets, contracts or field operations changed? Which were excluded? Obtain the actual instruments and subsequent observations before recording a stop or cancellation.
6. **Three legs at particular sites.** The Aditjondro/coali­tion typology is established as a research framework. Original ownership/role/payment records are still required for each proposed military-business relationship. Spatial overlap does not by itself establish a deployment's motive.
7. **Wabu/Intan Jaya.** Refresh permits, business histories, retirement/office dates and current project phase. Do not describe a planned or disputed concession as an operating mine without evidence. Retrieve original material behind the coalition report.
8. **Haris/Fatia proceedings.** The court-hosted analysis identifies Haris's PN/MA dockets, but both original decisions and Fatia's separate final docket need to be obtained for a complete legal file. Their acquittals are not blanket adjudication of every underlying business claim.
9. **Weapons chain.** The original CAR report and Serbian diplomatic note were not obtained. Several transfer/receipt/authorisation/use details remain expressly unconfirmed in Reuters' account. Identify later corroboration and preserve any unresolved last-mile link.
10. **Drone and chemical-agent identification.** A national sale, visual resemblance, a witness description and forensic identification are different evidence. Do not assign a manufacturer, payload, user or chemical agent without the relevant incident-specific support.
11. **Surveillance targeting.** Amnesty's Indonesian export/deployment research did not identify specific targets. Device forensics or similarly specific evidence is needed to connect a product to a particular Papuan person or operation.
12. **Disinformation sponsorship.** Public technical/network evidence may identify coordination or likely operators; ultimate clients/funders require their own records. Archive primary findings and vendor/platform responses.
13. **Freeport money definitions.** Keep direct support, overhead, in-kind provision, allowances and internal security separate. Refresh later reporting before stating current totals. Company-wide figures cannot automatically be attributed entirely to Papua.
14. **Freeport political/ownership history.** Obtain the original 2015 ethics materials and later legal/ethical outcomes, dated ownership/transaction instruments, and state/regional entitlements. Allegations, denials, proposed transfers and completed transactions remain distinct.
15. **Tangguh finance.** The JBIC commitment is well documented; actual drawdowns, full cofinancing, later project phases and precise responsibility for particular community effects need additional records.
16. **Regional corruption cases.** Pagawak's first-instance result was found; later appeal/final status was not established. Any additional named case needs its actual judgments and subsequent dispositions. A KPK prevention statement does not identify a convicted actor or quantified criminal loss.
17. **Shutdown litigation.** Acquire and compare the original 2020 administrative and 2021 constitutional decisions. They concern different proceedings; avoid presenting them as a single undifferentiated legal result.
18. **CIA-specific history.** The attempted CIA document returned a system-maintenance page. FRUS and the National Security Archive support diplomatic research; specific CIA operations/corporate connections require their own documentary proof. A scholar's thesis remains an attributed argument until its sources are checked.
19. **Historical quotations.** The April 2021 MPR quotation has a primary institutional source. The circulating Ali Murtopo quotation needs an earliest source, exact edition/page, context and indication of whether it is recollection or direct recording. Do not publish a viral attribution as verified wording.
20. **Papuan thought and current organisational mandates.** Acquire passages from the identified Papuan works and current original organisational statements. IPAC's 2015 overview is historical, not a 2026 leadership chart. Keep differences in political strategy and mandate visible.
21. **Public-data access.** AHU has company/beneficial-owner search surfaces, but historical profiles, bulk use, access restrictions and reporting completeness need checking. KPU summary data is not a full donor dataset; BPK press statements are not the full audit reports. Avoid promising automatic comprehensive ingestion before inspecting each source.
