# First six dossiers — evidence deepening and implementation

26 October 2026. This follows the initial twenty-thread research programme. All twenty threads are preserved in the original brief/queue and in the public preview's Research index. They are not twenty completed investigations.

The first interactive record contains six edited case files, 42 supported claims, 56 participating entities and 34 registered source/reading entries. Claims shared between files are stored once. Source counts are not independent-confirmation counts. The original 42-entry Merauke chronology remains available at its existing route; the new connections are a curated selection, not a replacement chronology.

## 1. Merauke

**New original evidence:** the seven-page Presidential Decree 15/2024 and Cabinet Secretariat explanation. Articles 1–3 identify the integrated sugar/bioethanol/biomass programme, presidential responsibility and land/licensing/investment-facilitation tasks. Article 5's OCR was checked against the official explanatory text; the chair is the investment minister/head of BKPM. Articles 9–11 concern organisation, quarterly reporting and task-force costs. These are not a disclosure of every plantation/road/rice project's capital and contracts.

**Public record:** decree and dated officeholder; Pusaka's clearing entry and Yei objection; Jhonlin/SANY order; presidential Wanam visit; the reported Malind road-permit judgment and advocates' response. Shared participants allow a reader to move to political access without inventing a common ownership chain.

**Next evidence:** original road judgment and appeal; historical deeds and permit instruments; procurement and funding documents; machinery financing/delivery; actual effects of the October 2026 forest-for-rice announcement. GPA ownership is not assigned to Salim/Djarum/Hartono from unverified search matches.

## 4. Freeport

**New disclosure:** the 2025 VPSHR report, retrieved in full text and read in targeted PTFI Grasberg sections, reports US$21m direct government-security support plus US$2m overhead, totalling US$23m. It describes 68% of overall support as in-kind, 56% directed to police, with the remainder directed to military units. Internal civilian security is a separate US$59m category. The public record retains the 2024 US$26m support figure as a separate period.

The report identifies renewal of national police and military MOUs in July and May 2025 respectively, each for five years. A separate October 2025 provincial-police arrangement concerns downstream processing. Do not merge the latter into Grasberg or infer the full agreement text from the report's summary. The company's stated no-munitions/no-command provisions remain attributed company positions.

The case also includes Yosepha Alomang's organising and Beanal's litigation. The US appeal names FCX entities, not PTFI as a named defendant. Its dismissal is described through legal pleading grounds rather than as a finding that all alleged harms never occurred. Current ownership percentages are not frozen from a historical filing.

**Next evidence:** contracts/IUPK and transaction instruments, regional entitlements, tax/royalty/dividend accounts, actual security agreements where public, environmental and labour records, dated grievances and outcomes. Earlier history remains available through the existing full case/History rather than being claimed as complete in this bounded 1999–2025 selection.

## 6. Information control

**New original text:** the complete 280-page reproduction of PTUN Jakarta judgment 230/G/TF/2019/PTUN-JKT, hosted by Columbia, was retrieved. Parties, concluding legal/proportionality reasoning and the operative order were reviewed; not all testimony or every cited exhibit was audited. The original court registry returned an access-denied page.

The order distinguishes 19 August 2019 throttling, the broad shutdown from 21 August, and later extensions in six city/regency jurisdictions. It declares the specified actions of the minister and president unlawful. The reasoning discusses the president's approval/omission of emergency procedures. The decision was deliberated on 28 May and pronounced on 3 June 2020. The latter is the timeline date used. Geographic descriptions retain the 2019 provincial structure.

The later 2021 Constitutional Court litigation addresses statutory blocking authority; it is not presented as the same proceeding. Bellingcat's published information-operation investigation and Amnesty's national spyware research remain related reading with distinct attribution/targeting questions, not invented Papuan victim-device links.

**Next evidence:** original later constitutional decision, implementation and further restrictions, technical measurements and reporting effects; ultimate influence-network clients; specific consensually public forensic targeting findings.

## Interface and continuity decisions

- Native SVG with preset positions suits the current small edited neighbourhoods. No graph package, continuous force simulation, per-query inference or WebGL context is required. A materially larger dataset should be profiled before changing renderer.
- Desktop has a case index, scene and reader. Phone/tablet use a focused relationship with local previous/next controls plus the complete readable list. The local controls do not change the selected investigation.
- Search resolves names/aliases, cases and claims; person and office identities remain separate. Selecting a common entity/claim provides links to the other files containing it.
- The six accounts and 42 claims also enter the existing build-time site retrieval corpus, with original source URLs and locators attached to claim records. The static search and Pages fallback retrieval include them. A bound Watch Engine uses its bundled corpus and needs its next normal rebuild/deployment before its Ask answers include these additions; the preview does not redeploy that production worker. No model, quota, schedule or database schema changes are needed. This does not turn Ask into an autonomous investigator or publisher.
- Connections, timeline, sources, case/entity/claim and source-sheet state have shareable URLs and browser Back. Invalid or stale case-specific IDs are discarded. Source inspection exposes the exact cited clause/section where available.
- Existing topic URLs, the complete Merauke chronology, Homepage Atlas, History and automated news/weekly pipelines remain. The topic directory is retained below the workspace; no-JavaScript reading includes all six accounts and their supported claims.
- Taste Skill is vendored and pinned, with integration guidance in `docs/TASTE_INTEGRATION.md`. Brand exceptions are explicit. Browser measurements are aids, not proof of award quality or physical-device performance.
- Still pending: a global large-network overview, deeper original corporate/financial document retrieval, precise incident/concession geometry, cross-site reverse citations, saved research selections and reviewed AI-assisted intake. No arbitrary inferred allegations or generative executable UI have been added.
