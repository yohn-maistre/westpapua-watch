# West Papua Watch — investigative research programme

26 October 2026. Research and information-structure planning only. No application, UI, branch, deployment or production-data changes.

This extends the existing experience plans. The user's direction is a Papuan-centred investigative public record connecting resources, money, political authority, military power, historical decisions, coercion, ideas and Papuan action. The purpose of this pass is to identify substantive investigations and a coherent way to connect their evidence before choosing the interface.

The [source register](SOURCE-REGISTER.md) records 43 retrieved documents/pages and one unavailable CIA lead. Reading was targeted: several long reports/PDFs were only partly inspected. [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) gives scope and limitations, and [SEARCH-LOG.json](SEARCH-LOG.json) preserves discovery leads, including weak/rejected results. This is a substantial initial research map, not a completed investigation of every named person or all publicly available Papua data.

## The central editorial proposition

Watch can explain how decisions about Papua are made, financed, implemented, contested and experienced. A named company or politician is one entry into that account; it is not the whole account. Each investigation should connect a specific place and period with the institutions, transactions, decisions and people for which evidence exists.

The recurring questions are:

- Who can authorise a project, classify land, issue a permit, move funds or deploy institutional power?
- Which companies, public bodies, investors, suppliers, intermediaries and officeholders participate, and in what period?
- What money, material or economic benefit is actually recorded, and on what accounting basis?
- What do documents, satellite observations, witnesses and investigations establish about what happened?
- Which Papuans consented, objected, organised, litigated, negotiated or proposed another course?
- What happened to complaints, investigations, judgments, reforms and announced remedies?
- Which historical decisions explain today's institutions and disputes, and which proposed connections remain unproven?

The user also explicitly wants Indonesian corruption/collusion and political enrichment included. That belongs at the core: parties, political finance, appointments, public spending, concessions, state-owned enterprises, tax/subsidy arrangements and business networks. Papuan officeholders belong in the same accountability framework. Public decisions, interests, evidence and outcomes determine the record, rather than an ethnic or loyalty classification.

## Strong starting findings from this pass

### Military business has an explicit research framework

The August 2021 coalition report *Ekonomi-Politik Penempatan Militer di Papua: Kasus Intan Jaya* explicitly cites George Junus Aditjondro's three legs: institutional business through foundations/cooperatives, non-institutional business through officers' roles in civilian/family companies, and grey/informal coercive business. The original Aditjondro reference is *Wacana* XVII/2004, pp.84–87; that original text was not obtained here. The framework should guide questions, not assign a specific business practice to an actor without case evidence. [S01](https://trendasia.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Ekonomi-Politik-Penempatan-Militer-di-Papua.pdf)

HRW's 2006 research provides a separate historical analysis of military-owned ventures, private alliances, illicit activity and corruption. Use it with dated company/legislative records when following changes over time. [S02](https://www.hrw.org/reports/2006/indonesia0606/5.htm)

### Some financial links can be documented directly

Freeport's 2024 Voluntary Principles report records US$24 million in direct government-security support costs plus US$2 million overhead. It describes much support as in-kind. The total must not be rendered as US$26 million in cash or as a payment for a particular attack. [S04](https://www.voluntaryprinciples.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Freeport-McMoRan-2024-VPSHR-Annual-Report.pdf)

JBIC's own 2016 notice records an agreement for up to US$1.2 billion, its portion of financing for Tangguh LNG 3, through Japan International Finance Management (Tangguh) Corporation. It identifies Japanese sponsors and an LNG offtake arrangement. This is a commitment/loan-limit record, not proof that the whole amount was disbursed or paid directly to BP. [S16](https://www.jbic.go.jp/en/information/press/press-2016/0705-49355.html)

### A supplier-to-Papua weapons investigation already has an anchor

Reuters reported CAR's tracing of Serbian-made mortar rounds through Krusik, Zenitprom and PT Pindad for BIN, with evidence relating to attacks in Papua. It explicitly could not establish several transfer details, purchase authorisation or who used the weapons. Keep those qualifications attached to the relevant links; the original CAR report and diplomatic note were not obtained in this pass. [S05](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/mystery-surrounds-how-munitions-imported-indonesias-civilian-spies-were-used-2022-06-03/)

HRM's Kiwirok investigation supplies a distinct route into reported civilian effects and satellite/witness evidence. It does not make every later weapons claim established fact. [S06](https://humanrightsmonitor.org/reports/kiwirok-report-2023/)

### Merauke connects policy, machinery, finance and land authority

SANY's June 2024 announcement records a Jhonlin order for 2,000 excavators and identifies the signing owner. The announcement alone does not establish its price, financing source or deployment of every machine. [S08](https://www.sanyglobal.com/press_releases/2917/)

The presidential account of the November 2024 Wanam visit and subsequent project reporting provide routes into the government's programme and the roles of operators and infrastructure. The different rice, sugar/bioethanol and road projects need distinct records. [S09](https://presidenri.go.id/siaran-pers/presiden-prabowo-tinjau-infrastruktur-areal-lumbung-pangan-di-wanam-merauke/) · [S10](https://news.mongabay.com/2025/11/with-military-backing-and-oligarch-allies-indonesia-pushes-controversial-food-estate/)

Tempo's accessible reporting raises appointment/lobbying questions around Haji Isam's network. That is a research route; it does not supply a verified campaign-donation amount or prove a quid pro quo. [S11](https://www.tempo.co/politik/menteri-prabowo-orang-haji-isam-1543228)

### Public political statements are researchable records

The MPR's own April 26, 2021 statement records Bambang Soesatyo saying: “Tumpas habis dulu. Urusan HAM kita bicarakan kemudian.” Its speaker, office, date, publication and immediate context can be recorded. The statement alone does not establish an operational order for an attack months later elsewhere. [S35](https://mpr.go.id/berita/Satu-Lagi,-Kabinda-Papua-Gugur-Ditembak-KKB-Papua.-Bamsoet-Minta-TNI-Polri-Turunkan-Kekuatan-Penuh-Tumpas-KKB)

### Current policy statements create specific follow-up questions

October 2026 reporting describes Hashim Djojohadikusumo's announcement that further forest expansion for rice was unnecessary, alongside unresolved implications for existing projects and a more qualified Forestry Ministry response. Follow decrees, permits, budgets, contracts and observed clearing to establish implementation. Do not convert an announcement into a recorded cancellation. [S38](https://news.mongabay.com/2026/10/indonesia-says-it-no-longer-needs-forests-for-rice-what-happens-to-projects-underway/)

## Twenty connected investigative threads

These are an editorial research structure, not a proposed twenty-item navigation menu. Threads share records; named cases bundle them into a readable account.

### 1. Merauke: authority, operators and beneficiaries

**Question:** Who authorises, implements, finances and benefits from each food, energy and infrastructure project, and whose land decisions are overridden or recognised?

Start with the existing Pusaka chronology and separate rice, sugar/bioethanol, road, public works and finance records. Follow presidential/ministry decisions, government task forces, Jhonlin's machinery/contractor role, public operators, permitting authorities, individual concession companies and affected Malind/Yei and other specific communities. Original land/permit instruments and financing records remain essential. [S08–S11, S19, S39]

Haji Isam is an important research subject under his canonical identity, Andi Syamsuddin Arsyad, with source-specific spelling variants retained. Resolve the Jhonlin business group and each incorporated subsidiary separately. Do not assume every Merauke company belongs to him.

**Next records:** AHU/deeds and shareholder histories; procurement/contract instruments; budget appropriations and actual payments; machinery finance; environmental approvals and maps; court decisions; community statements and the documented implementation of remedies.

### 2. Political finance, appointments and business access

**Question:** How do Indonesian party/campaign networks and business interests intersect with decisions affecting Papua?

Begin with KPU disclosures, party/ticket structures, ministerial appointments, public business roles and specific decisions. JATAM's 2024 report is a national discovery aid; its company affiliations must be checked against original records. The read KPU summary contains aggregate receipts/spending, not a complete donor-level explanation. [S11–S13]

Prabowo, his administration, family-linked business networks, party financiers, coalition politicians and conglomerate principals belong in this research universe when records establish their roles. Board appointments, business ownership, political support, disclosed donations, alleged off-book support and corrupt payments are different relationships.

**Next records:** full LPPDK/donor schedules and audits; dated company records; LHKPN declarations; appointment decisions; policy drafts; tender/contract records; relevant corruption proceedings and responses. No donor amount should be invented from a report of political closeness.

### 3. Three legs of military business

**Question:** Through what institutional, private and informal arrangements does military/police power participate in economic activity?

Use the Aditjondro/coali­tion framework and HRW's historical work to organise dated cases: foundations/cooperatives, officers' corporate posts, security arrangements and documented coercive or illicit activity. Intan Jaya/Wabu is an important dossier, but a concession/deployment overlay is a research lead rather than automatic proof of motive. [S01–S02]

Follow reform commitments, business transfers, retirement status, active-duty appointments, public agencies and actual company relationships over time. Keep TNI, Polri, BIN, individual units and ministries distinct.

**Next records:** foundation/cooperative corporate histories, annual reports, formal support agreements, court findings, verified payment records, business-reform regulations and dated personnel roles.

### 4. Freeport: contracts, security, ownership and public revenue

**Question:** How have mining rights, economic control, security support, revenue and land relations changed from the contract era to the current structure?

Connect the 1967 and later contracts, extraction phases, PTFI/FCX and state ownership, fiscal terms, disclosed security support and Amungme/Kamoro accounts. Company filings are useful primary disclosures; pair their stated policies with independent research and community records. [S03–S04, S30, S36, S40]

The 2015 parliamentary share-extortion allegations and denial, recorded in Reuters reporting, provide a separate political-business proceeding to investigate. The allegations are not interchangeable with a corruption conviction in another case. [S43](https://www.reuters.com/article/markets/commodities/freeport-indonesia-head-says-speaker-of-parliament-tried-to-extort-shares-idUSL3N13T1WX/) · [S44](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/speaker-of-indonesian-parliament-denies-freeport-extortion-allegation-idUSKBN0TQ1Q1/) Preserve the hearing, disputed recording, responses and later legal/ethical outcomes.

**Next records:** contract/IUPK texts, transaction documents, annual/security reports, MIND ID and regional entitlement records, tax/royalty/dividend accounts, environmental monitoring, labour proceedings, original ethics hearings and court dispositions.

### 5. Tangguh: gas, lenders, export buyers and affected communities

**Question:** Who finances the project, holds economic interests, buys the output and bears its land, livelihood and environmental effects?

JBIC's named borrower vehicle, loan, sponsors and offtake are a strong starting financial record. Extend to BP/other sponsors, additional lenders, export-credit support, project phases, resettlement agreements and community accounts. [S16]

**Next records:** ADB/JBIC project documents, environmental/social assessments and monitoring, sponsor reports, debt agreements where public, offtake terms, grievance records and local testimony. A lender's participation is a financing fact; a claim of complicity requires its own evidence.

### 6. Plantation permits, opaque ownership and commodity buyers

**Question:** How do permits and corporate structures turn customary forests into plantations, and where do capital and products move?

Tanah Merah, Awyu land cases, Sorong-area plantations and the historical Salim/Indogunta research offer specific starts. Track the concession-holding company, owners and intermediaries as they changed, alongside the permit issuer and community objections. [S17–S19, S29]

**Next records:** historical company deeds, permit signatures and amendments, court records, mill/refinery links, buyer disclosures and grievance processes. A brand's indirect supply exposure differs from ownership, operational control or a direct purchase.

No reliable Global Papua Abadi–Djarum/Hartono/Salim ownership link was established in this pass. Search noise must not become a public edge.

### 7. State projects, procurement and enrichment

**Question:** Who plans, awards, builds, invoices and is paid for public projects, and what was delivered?

Roads, ports, airports, energy facilities, security infrastructure and food-estate works can connect APBN/APBD budgets, ministries, local authorities, BUMN, prime contractors and subcontractors. Distinguish the budget ceiling, procurement plan, award, contract, variation, invoice and actual disbursement.

**Next records:** SiRUP/LPSE/INAPROC records and document access where available, contract amendments, BPK/BPKP findings, beneficial ownership and litigation. Being awarded a contract is not by itself evidence of corruption; a documented irregularity needs its own record.

### 8. Weapons, export chains and the last mile to an incident

**Question:** What can be established about the manufacture, transfer, procurement and actual use of weapons in specific Papua incidents?

Kiwirok provides a credible starting chain through the Reuters/CAR account and separate civilian-impact research. Preserve each link's source and missing last-mile evidence. [S05–S07]

**Next records:** original tracing report/note verbale if publicly obtainable, export permits/end-user records, procurement disclosures, verified imagery and independent technical identification. A national sale to Indonesia is not proof of use in Papua. Photographs and accounts of burning do not by themselves identify a chemical agent.

### 9. Operations, displacement and disruption of civilian life

**Question:** What happened in a specific campaign or incident, which actors were involved, and how did people live and organise afterward?

Connect existing History research on the 1960s, highlands operations, Timika, Biak, Wasior, Wamena, Paniai and more recent Nduga/Intan Jaya/Kiwirok records. Retain specific dates, units where established, communities, witness accounts, inquiry findings, displacement and service interruptions. [S06–S07 plus existing History evidence ledgers]

**Next records:** original Komnas HAM/ELSHAM/church inquiries, medical/service records, satellite assessments, humanitarian monitoring and follow-up accounts. Different casualty estimates and direct/indirect deaths must remain distinguishable. Do not infer an individual's command responsibility from a job title alone.

### 10. Detention, abduction, assassination and accountability

**Question:** How were people targeted, what happened to inquiries and prosecutions, and which questions remain unresolved?

Theys Eluay/Aristoteles Masoka, Arnold Ap, political-prisoner cases, 2019 protest prosecutions and environmental-defender proceedings are researchable case families. Distinguish a survivor account, investigation, indictment, acquittal, conviction, appeal and reparation.

Haris/Fatia's prosecution belongs to the connected history of investigating military-business allegations. A court-hosted 2025 account identifies the Supreme Court decision affirming Haris's acquittal; retrieve each defendant's original judgment separately. An acquittal of the researchers does not automatically establish every business allegation. [S24–S25]

### 11. Internet restrictions and information access

**Question:** Who ordered or implemented communications restrictions, under what legal claims, and with what documented effects?

The 2019 shutdown, 2020 administrative judgment and 2021 constitutional ruling must remain separate events and proceedings. The available sources show why a single undated 'the courts ruled' label would mislead. [S20–S21]

**Next records:** original decisions, government directions, operators' disclosures, network measurements, journalists' accounts and effects on reporting, education, commerce and emergency access. Do not treat a technical outage as intentional censorship without evidence.

### 12. Propaganda and coordinated influence

**Question:** Which narratives, account networks, vendors and disclosed clients shaped information about Papua?

The BBC/ASPI investigation published through Bellingcat offers a concrete 2019 network investigation and a documented methodology. [S23]

**Next records:** platform takedown disclosures, archived public posts, domain/ad transparency data, procurement and vendor responses. Technical coordination, likely operator and ultimate sponsor have different evidence requirements. Preserve material without amplifying unverified accusations or targeting ordinary private users.

### 13. Surveillance vendors, agencies and targeting

**Question:** Which systems were sold or deployed, by whom, under what authority, and can particular targeting be established?

Amnesty's 2024 technical work traces an Indonesian vendor/broker/agency ecosystem but expressly did not identify specific targets. Treat a Papua deployment/targeting claim as a separate research question. [S22]

**Next records:** original export/trade documents, agency procurement and forensic victim-device findings where consensually public. An export to Indonesia cannot silently become 'this tool surveilled this Papuan'.

### 14. Otsus, territorial administration and elite networks

**Question:** How do changes in autonomy, provincial boundaries, appointments and spending redistribute authority and resources?

Connect the law and MRP/DPRP roles, territorial expansion, public budgets, contracts and service delivery. The July 2026 KPK statement identifies governance risk areas but is not a criminal judgment. ANTARA's report on Pagawak provides a first-instance court event; later legal status needs verification. [S26–S28]

**Next records:** laws and implementing instruments, court challenges, transfer/allocation data, APBD realisation, audit findings, procurement and original corruption judgments. Include Papuan officials' public choices, interests and criticisms on the same evidentiary basis as national actors.

### 15. Land authority, consent, women and social relations

**Question:** Who is treated as able to consent to a land transaction, and whose use, care and authority are excluded?

HRW's South Papua field research makes women's accounts and decision-making a substantive investigation. The Malind road case gives a connected permit/legal-response record. [S29, S39]

**Next records:** the actual agreement and signatories' roles, boundaries and collective decision processes, compensation terms, women's accounts and ecological/food knowledge. A leader's signature does not automatically establish every rights-holder's consent. Adat practices require local specificity.

### 16. Work, wages, strikes and company-community institutions

**Question:** How are extraction benefits and risks distributed among workers and surrounding communities, and how are rights contested?

Freeport's 2017 dispute has a contemporaneous union record. Extend to company responses, labour decisions, contractor employment and social-program institutions. [S34]

**Next records:** employment/collective agreements, dismissal and reinstatement proceedings, benefit/health arrangements, accident investigations, social-fund governance and community representation. A social programme is not automatically consent, compensation for all harm or a corrupt payment.

### 17. Land change, food, water and health

**Question:** Which changes are observed, what effects are documented, and who bears them?

Join geospatial observations to specific permits and places, then examine water/food systems, displacement and services through appropriate research. Nusantara Atlas distinguishes primary forest, other natural ecosystems and total clearing; retain those definitions. [S19, S29]

**Next records:** methods and underlying satellite dates, environmental/health studies, AMDAL monitoring, community knowledge, complaints and agency/company responses. Proximity or chronology alone does not establish a particular health cause. Fire detection, smoke model and health diagnosis are separate records.

### 18. Decolonisation, Cold War policy and resource rights

**Question:** How did Dutch, Indonesian, US and UN decisions shape Papuan political participation and the allocation of authority?

The FRUS memorandum and National Security Archive collection support a strong diplomatic investigation linking policy arguments, negotiations, exclusion/representation and the 1969 process. Connect extraction contracts only where documents support the specific relationship. [S14–S15]

**Next records:** original telegrams and memoranda, New York Agreement and UN records, Dutch cabinet/governor archives, Papuan petitions and institutional records. A CIA analysis is not evidence of a covert operation. The CIA URL attempted in this pass returned a maintenance page, so that document is still unread. [S41]

### 19. Foreign military relationships and civilian functions

**Question:** How do training, assistance, export policy and domestic military roles develop despite abuse findings and restrictions?

HRW's 2018 account offers an entry into US engagement/restrictions and unresolved accountability. The later military-law debate is another research lead requiring the enacted law, rather than a draft article alone. [S42]

**Next records:** government assistance/training notices, export records, enacted laws, official appointment decisions and court/inquiry outcomes. Separate a country's general relationship with Indonesia from a specific Papua incident.

### 20. Papuan political thought, organising, churches and cultural work

**Question:** What futures have Papuans argued for, through which institutions and strategies, and how have those positions changed?

Read Papuan-authored works and public statements alongside organisational histories: self-determination, dialogue, adat/land authority, church justice work, nonviolent organising, armed liberation, international diplomacy, autonomy and feminist/ecological positions. These are overlapping ideas and strategies, not rigid camps.

Tebay's authored dialogue proposal, Giay's catalogued *Menuju Papua Baru*, Karma's published account, Yosepha's organising and current community statements provide different entry points. IPAC's 2015 overview helps identify historical organisations but cannot serve as a current leadership map. [S30–S33 plus bibliography leads]

**Next records:** books/passages, recorded speeches and interviews, organisation statements with dates and mandates, church/community archives, cultural works and internal critiques. ULMWP, TPNPB, KNPB, OPM and smaller organisations require period-specific histories; shared goals do not establish a single command structure. Preserve Papuan perspectives and disagreements rather than treating everyone as a generic victim or organisation node.

## Recommended first substantive research slate

Develop six connected dossiers first, while keeping Papuan voices and responses within every one:

1. **Merauke decisions, machinery and finance.** The current chronology supplies a base; deepen the company/permit/contract/capital record and unresolved consent questions.
2. **Military business, using Intan Jaya/Wabu and disclosed Freeport support as distinct cases.** Test the three-leg framework with real records rather than an all-purpose overlay.
3. **Freeport's changing rights, security arrangements and economic benefits.** A long record and company disclosures can connect history with current accountability.
4. **Kiwirok weapons and civilian consequences.** A bounded incident-level tracing case with explicit gaps and distinct technical/human-rights evidence.
5. **Indonesian political capital and Papua-facing decisions.** Begin with Haji Isam/Jhonlin's documented roles and the broader party/appointment/company record; pursue primary finance evidence before drawing donation arrows.
6. **Communications control and accountability.** Combine the shutdown's legal sequence with separately documented information operations; keep specific surveillance targeting in its own evidence lane.

Tangguh and Tanah Merah/plantation supply chains are strong next dossiers for testing international finance and opaque ownership. Otsus/procurement can become a continuing accountability programme. The Cold War file is a research spine linking existing History to present institutions, without changing History's UI now.

## The information structure

Use a shared record model beneath these files. The core is a **supported statement about a relationship, event, observation or transaction**, with its own date, source and scope. Named entities supply the participants. The [information-model note](INFORMATION-MODEL.md) specifies the record families, financial semantics, quotation/position handling and evidence states.

A dossier should always include a clear research question, a dated authored account, what is established, how the record changed, Papuan action/experience, disputed or unresolved points, and the next documents needed. Sources remain inspectable. Cross-links should reveal real shared records; they do not need invented lines to make every thread connected.

Public language can be explicit about documented violence, dispossession, corruption and coercion. Legal process and attribution remain visible where they affect the claim. That supports a Papuan-centred account of power with enough precision for researchers to reuse.

## Boundaries of this pass

This pass identifies and grounds a research programme. It does not establish a complete current beneficial-ownership graph, a full donor ledger, every weapon's chain of custody, a current command map or the legal status of every case. The [open-questions list](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md) records the consequential gaps. The UI and existing public content remain unchanged.
