## Reading and editorial decisions

- Every account has one owning path. Short related-account links connect histories without repeating whole accounts across paths.
- Political history opens with western coastal relations and colonial expansion. Customary leadership, language, population histories and schools have their own society path; Sahul remains in early settlement.
- Local arrows cycle eligible accounts within the selected path. Distinct labels identify Council/Diaspora, Authority/Exchange, Landscape/Sahul and other alternatives.
- Specialist accounts formerly attached to political periods now use their own dates/context labels. Undated finds remain undated. Recent accounts close the political path after the dated episodes.
- Timika 1994–1995, Mapenduma/Geselema, Biak, Abepura, Theys Eluay, Wamena and Nduga are new accounts. Wasior and recent aerial attacks become visible political sections rather than optional facets hidden behind another event.
- Pepera describes controlled participation, death threats and suppression directly. The highlands account describes bombing, strafing, torture, sexual violence and displacement using the investigation's own evidence and dated victim list.
- TPNPB is named as the West Papua National Liberation Army and its independence purpose is stated. The account distinguishes armed commands, civilian mobilisation and diplomatic coalitions. Documented civilian harm and hostage-taking are retained.
- Prabowo's documented command at Mapenduma is stated. This is not extended to unrelated incidents, unverified personal orders or responsibility for every gunman. Biak casualty categories remain separate; disputed weapons identification remains attributed.
- Court outcomes are attached to the relevant episodes. The Paniai account ends with the December 2022 acquittal and criticism of the limited prosecution, without asserting an unverified subsequent appeal outcome.
- The user's family recollection is not published; only the independently documented Amungme court filings are included. An unidentified green-flag organisation is not given a speculative identity.

Each paragraph carries source URLs rendered as inline numbered citations. Source rows remain visible. Redundant uncited references were removed; old citation hashes and photograph-account links have migration aliases. Canonical retained source anchors remain URL-derived and stable when reference order changes.
