## Pepera — 1969

### Indonesian

Perjanjian New York menjanjikan penentuan nasib sendiri bagi penduduk Papua sebelum akhir 1969. Ketika Pepera berlangsung pada Juli–Agustus, pemerintah Indonesia menggunakan delapan dewan musyawarah dengan sekitar seribu wakil pilihannya. Penduduk dewasa Papua tidak diberi kesempatan memberikan suara masing-masing. Pemerintah menyebut cara ini sesuai dengan keadaan setempat, sementara pilihan peserta dan jalannya musyawarah berada di bawah kendalinya. [1](https://www.ulmwp.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Thomas-Musgrave-An-analysis-of-the-1969-Act-of-Free-Choice-in-West-Papua-2015.pdf)

Peserta dipisahkan dari keluarga sebelum musyawarah. Kesaksian yang dihimpun dalam penelitian sejarah mencatat ancaman pembunuhan, tekanan dan pemberian imbalan; sebagian peserta diperintahkan melatih pernyataan yang akan mereka bacakan. Demonstrasi yang menuntut pemungutan suara langsung dibubarkan. Di Biak, wartawan Hugh Lunn menyaksikan tentara memilih wakil dan menangkap pengunjuk rasa di hadapan petugas PBB. Hasil seluruh dewan kemudian dinyatakan mendukung integrasi dengan Indonesia. [2](https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/papoeawestnieuwguinea/papers_pdf/saltford)

Tim PBB yang dipimpin Fernando Ortiz Sanz mengetahui ketatnya pengendalian politik tersebut. Dokumen diplomatik Amerika Serikat juga memperlihatkan bahwa pejabat asing mengetahui besarnya penolakan Papua dan memperkirakan Indonesia tidak akan membiarkan hasil yang mendukung kemerdekaan. Pemerintah-pemerintah yang berpengaruh memilih menjaga hubungan dengan Jakarta. Hak orang Papua untuk menentukan masa depan mereka ditempatkan di bawah kepentingan hubungan antarnegara. [2](https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/papoeawestnieuwguinea/papers_pdf/saltford) [3](https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB128/)

Pada 19 November, Resolusi Majelis Umum PBB 2504 mencatat laporan Sekretaris Jenderal mengenai proses itu. Tidak ada pemungutan suara baru bagi penduduk Papua. Indonesia menjadikan hasil Pepera dasar integrasi, sementara gerakan kemerdekaan Papua terus menuntut pemenuhan hak penentuan nasib sendiri yang telah dijanjikan. Pengendalian dan ancaman dalam Pepera menjadi bagian pokok tuntutan tersebut. [1](https://www.ulmwp.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Thomas-Musgrave-An-analysis-of-the-1969-Act-of-Free-Choice-in-West-Papua-2015.pdf) [4](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/202193) [5](https://www.files.ethz.ch/isn/26108/PS014.pdf)

### English

The New York Agreement promised an act of self-determination for Papua's inhabitants by the end of 1969. When Pepera took place in July and August, Indonesia used eight consultation assemblies with around a thousand selected representatives. Papuan adults were not allowed to cast individual votes. The government described this method as suited to local conditions, while controlling both the selection of participants and the proceedings. [1](https://www.ulmwp.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Thomas-Musgrave-An-analysis-of-the-1969-Act-of-Free-Choice-in-West-Papua-2015.pdf)

Representatives were separated from their families before the assemblies. Historical testimony records death threats, pressure and inducements; some participants were instructed to rehearse the statements they would deliver. Demonstrations demanding a direct vote were broken up. In Biak, journalist Hugh Lunn witnessed soldiers selecting representatives and arresting protesters in front of UN officials. Every assembly was subsequently declared to support integration with Indonesia. [2](https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/papoeawestnieuwguinea/papers_pdf/saltford)

The UN team led by Fernando Ortiz Sanz knew how tightly political activity was controlled. US diplomatic records also show that foreign officials recognised substantial Papuan opposition and expected Indonesia to prevent an outcome favouring independence. Influential governments chose to protect their relations with Jakarta. Papuans' right to determine their future was subordinated to relations between states. [2](https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/papoeawestnieuwguinea/papers_pdf/saltford) [3](https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB128/)

On 19 November, General Assembly Resolution 2504 took note of the Secretary-General's report on the process. There was no new vote for Papua's population. Indonesia treated Pepera's result as the basis of integration, while Papuan independence movements continued to demand the self-determination that had been promised. The control and threats surrounding Pepera became central to that demand. [1](https://www.ulmwp.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Thomas-Musgrave-An-analysis-of-the-1969-Act-of-Free-Choice-in-West-Papua-2015.pdf) [4](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/202193) [5](https://www.files.ethz.ch/isn/26108/PS014.pdf)

Editorial note: verify any exact participant count against the original assembly report; the studies reviewed use different totals. Do not infer population opinion percentages from a diplomat's estimate as though it were a poll.

## Operasi militer di pegunungan tengah / Military operations in the Central Highlands — 1977–1978

### Indonesian

Menjelang pemilihan umum 1977, penolakan terhadap pemerintahan Indonesia meluas di sejumlah wilayah pegunungan. Warga menolak mengikuti pemilu, sementara pasukan OPM menyerang pos dan merusak lapangan terbang. Militer Indonesia mengerahkan operasi darat dan udara. Serangan menjangkau kampung-kampung di sekitar Wamena, Baliem, Karubaga dan Bokondini, dengan warga sipil menjadi sasaran kekerasan. [1](https://tapol.org/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/pdfs/NeglectedGenocideAHRC.pdf)

Kesaksian penyintas mencatat pengeboman dan penembakan dari udara, pembakaran rumah, penangkapan, penyiksaan, kekerasan seksual dan pembunuhan. Warga yang tidak mengenali pesawat perang keluar ke tempat terbuka, mengira pesawat membawa bantuan. Penelitian Asian Human Rights Commission dan International Coalition for Papua juga menghubungkan serangan udara dengan pengerahan pesawat OV-10 Bronco serta helikopter melalui catatan militer dan laporan pada masa itu. [1](https://tapol.org/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/pdfs/NeglectedGenocideAHRC.pdf)

Keluarga meninggalkan rumah dan kebun untuk berlindung. Kelaparan dan penyakit menambah kematian di antara mereka yang selamat dari serangan langsung. Penyintas yang diwawancarai puluhan tahun kemudian masih mengingat orang tua, anak, pasangan dan tetangga yang hilang. Penyelidikan yang diterbitkan pada 2013 menghimpun 4.146 nama orang yang dilaporkan meninggal dari wawancara di 15 distrik; catatan tersebut tidak mencakup seluruh korban. [1](https://tapol.org/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/pdfs/NeglectedGenocideAHRC.pdf)

Pengungsian memutus kehidupan kampung dan akses terhadap pangan. Keluarga yang kembali harus hidup dengan kehilangan anggota keluarga dan kerusakan penghidupan, sementara penyintas terus menuntut pengungkapan kekerasan dan pertanggungjawaban atas operasi tersebut. [1](https://tapol.org/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/pdfs/NeglectedGenocideAHRC.pdf)

### English

Opposition to Indonesian rule spread in parts of the highlands before the 1977 election. Residents refused to participate, while OPM forces attacked posts and damaged airstrips. Indonesia's military deployed ground and aerial operations. Attacks reached villages around Wamena, the Baliem Valley, Karubaga and Bokondini, with civilians subjected to violence. [1](https://tapol.org/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/pdfs/NeglectedGenocideAHRC.pdf)

Survivors described aerial bombing and strafing, burned homes, arrests, torture, sexual violence and killings. Some residents who did not recognise warplanes went into the open, believing the aircraft carried assistance. Research by the Asian Human Rights Commission and International Coalition for Papua also connects the aerial attacks to OV-10 Bronco aircraft and helicopter deployments through military accounts and contemporary reporting. [1](https://tapol.org/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/pdfs/NeglectedGenocideAHRC.pdf)

Families left their homes and gardens to seek shelter. Hunger and disease caused further deaths among people who survived the direct attacks. Interviewed decades later, survivors still remembered parents, children, partners and neighbours they had lost. The investigation published in 2013 compiled 4,146 names of people reported killed from interviews in 15 districts; its record did not cover every victim. [1](https://tapol.org/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/pdfs/NeglectedGenocideAHRC.pdf)

Displacement broke up village life and access to food. Returning families faced the loss of relatives and damage to their livelihoods, while survivors continued to demand disclosure and accountability for the military operations. [1](https://tapol.org/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/pdfs/NeglectedGenocideAHRC.pdf)

Editorial note: the victim figure is the investigation's list, not a definitive census. Exact weapon-type claims and named command responsibilities require their specific passages. Do not add different estimates together.

## Biak Berdarah / Biak massacre — July 1998

### Indonesian

Setelah Suharto jatuh pada Mei 1998, tuntutan kemerdekaan disampaikan lebih terbuka di Papua. Pada 2 Juli, Bintang Kejora berkibar di menara air dekat pelabuhan Biak. Warga berkumpul, menyanyi dan menyampaikan tuntutan politik. Filep Karma menjadi salah satu tokoh utama perkumpulan itu. Aparat meminta bendera diturunkan, tetapi warga mempertahankannya. [1](https://www.hrw.org/legacy/reports/reports98/biak/biak.htm) [2](https://www.biak-tribunal.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Names-Without-Graves_Elsham-Papua-English.pdf)

Sekitar pukul lima pagi pada 6 Juli, pasukan menyerang dari beberapa arah dan menembaki warga. Human Rights Watch mencatat kesaksian tentang orang yang ditembak ketika sudah terbaring di tanah. Setelah serangan, warga ditangkap, dipukuli dan dibawa untuk ditahan. Korban luka menghadapi pembatasan akses rumah sakit; sejumlah jenazah tidak diserahkan kembali kepada keluarga. [1](https://www.hrw.org/legacy/reports/reports98/biak/biak.htm) [2](https://www.biak-tribunal.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Names-Without-Graves_Elsham-Papua-English.pdf)

ELSHAM bersama jaringan gereja mendokumentasikan delapan orang tewas, tiga orang hilang, puluhan orang luka dan penahanan. Laporan itu juga mencatat 32 jenazah tak dikenal yang ditemukan di perairan Biak. Keluarga yang kehilangan kerabat menghadapi ketidakpastian karena tubuh tidak dikembalikan dan orang hilang tidak ditemukan. Aparat mengaitkan jenazah di laut dengan tsunami di Papua Nugini; kesaksian warga mengenai kekerasan di Biak mendorong tuntutan penyelidikan lebih jauh. [2](https://www.biak-tribunal.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Names-Without-Graves_Elsham-Papua-English.pdf) [3](https://www.hrw.org/legacy/reports/reports98/biak/bodies.htm)

Biak memperlihatkan bahwa pembukaan ruang politik setelah Suharto tetap disertai kekerasan terhadap tuntutan kemerdekaan Papua. Dalam laporan 1999, ELSHAM meminta intimidasi terhadap keluarga dihentikan, penghilangan diungkap dan pelaku kekerasan diproses. Keluarga dan penyintas harus memperjuangkan pengakuan atas apa yang terjadi sekaligus mencari kepastian mengenai orang-orang yang mereka kehilangan. [2](https://www.biak-tribunal.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Names-Without-Graves_Elsham-Papua-English.pdf)

### English

After Suharto fell in May 1998, demands for independence were voiced more openly in Papua. On 2 July, the Morning Star flag flew from the water tower near Biak's harbour. Residents gathered, sang and made political demands. Filep Karma became a leading figure in the gathering. Security officers asked for the flag to be lowered, but participants maintained it. [1](https://www.hrw.org/legacy/reports/reports98/biak/biak.htm) [2](https://www.biak-tribunal.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Names-Without-Graves_Elsham-Papua-English.pdf)

At around five in the morning on 6 July, troops attacked from several directions and fired on the crowd. Human Rights Watch recorded testimony that people were shot while already lying on the ground. After the attack, residents were arrested, beaten and taken into detention. Wounded people faced restrictions on hospital access; some bodies were not returned to their families. [1](https://www.hrw.org/legacy/reports/reports98/biak/biak.htm) [2](https://www.biak-tribunal.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Names-Without-Graves_Elsham-Papua-English.pdf)

ELSHAM, working with church networks, documented eight people killed, three missing, dozens injured and detentions. Its report separately recorded 32 unidentified bodies found in Biak's waters. Families were left without certainty as bodies were withheld and missing relatives remained unaccounted for. Authorities attributed the bodies at sea to the tsunami in Papua New Guinea; local testimony about violence in Biak prompted demands for further investigation. [2](https://www.biak-tribunal.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Names-Without-Graves_Elsham-Papua-English.pdf) [3](https://www.hrw.org/legacy/reports/reports98/biak/bodies.htm)

Biak showed that the political opening after Suharto was accompanied by continuing violence against Papuan independence demands. In its 1999 report, ELSHAM called for intimidation of families to end, disappearances to be investigated and perpetrators to be brought to justice. Families and survivors faced a struggle for recognition of what happened alongside the search for relatives they had lost. [2](https://www.biak-tribunal.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Names-Without-Graves_Elsham-Papua-English.pdf)

Editorial note: keep the report's casualty categories separate. Final publication should add verified later accountability developments and survivor organising, rather than implying this account establishes legal status in October 2026.

## MIFEE — 2010–2013

### Indonesian

Program Merauke Integrated Food and Energy Estate, MIFEE, diluncurkan pada 2010 untuk mengembangkan produksi pangan dan energi melalui perkebunan serta usaha berskala besar. Wilayah yang direncanakan mencakup tanah adat Malind, dengan hutan sagu, tempat berburu dan sungai yang menopang kehidupan kampung. Perundingan perusahaan dengan pemegang hak tanah membawa keputusan mengenai pangan, penghasilan dan hubungan antarmarga ke dalam penguasaan konsesi. [1](https://www.forestpeoples.org/fileadmin/uploads/fpp/migration/publication/2013/10/asweetnessundodeathmifeeindonesiaenglish2.pdf)

Penelitian lapangan FPP, Pusaka dan Sawit Watch pada 2013 menghimpun pengalaman warga Baad, Wayau, Koa dan Zanegi. Warga menjelaskan informasi yang tidak lengkap, janji bantuan dan pekerjaan, ketentuan ganti rugi yang ditetapkan sepihak, serta kesepakatan yang tidak jelas. Perundingan melalui sebagian pemimpin juga memecah keputusan bersama masyarakat adat. Anggota keluarga yang bergantung pada tanah dapat menanggung akibat keputusan tanpa memahami seluruh isi kesepakatannya. [1](https://www.forestpeoples.org/fileadmin/uploads/fpp/migration/publication/2013/10/asweetnessundodeathmifeeindonesiaenglish2.pdf)

Di Zanegi, penyerahan tanah kepada PT Selaras Inti Semesta, anak perusahaan Medco, sudah berlangsung pada 2009, sebelum peluncuran MIFEE. Warga kemudian melaporkan berkurangnya tempat berburu dan ikan. Peneliti mencatat kekurangan gizi dan penyakit pada anak, sementara pekerjaan berupah belum menggantikan pangan yang sebelumnya diperoleh dari hutan dan sungai. Kunjungan dokter yang dilaporkan hanya sehari dalam sebulan memperlihatkan terbatasnya layanan ketika kebutuhan kesehatan meningkat. [1](https://www.forestpeoples.org/fileadmin/uploads/fpp/migration/publication/2013/10/asweetnessundodeathmifeeindonesiaenglish2.pdf)

Kehilangan akses terhadap hutan mengubah kemampuan keluarga memperoleh makanan dan mempertahankan kehidupan di tanahnya. Janji pembangunan dihadapkan pada pengalaman warga yang menjadi lebih bergantung pada penghasilan tunai, makanan yang dibeli dan keputusan perusahaan. Catatan kampung-kampung ini menjelaskan dasar tuntutan perlindungan hak tanah dan persetujuan masyarakat dalam proyek-proyek berikutnya di Merauke. [1](https://www.forestpeoples.org/fileadmin/uploads/fpp/migration/publication/2013/10/asweetnessundodeathmifeeindonesiaenglish2.pdf)

### English

The Merauke Integrated Food and Energy Estate programme, MIFEE, was launched in 2010 to develop food and energy production through plantations and large-scale enterprises. The proposed area included Malind customary lands, with sago forests, hunting grounds and rivers sustaining village life. Company negotiations over land rights brought decisions about food, income and relationships between clans into concession arrangements. [1](https://www.forestpeoples.org/fileadmin/uploads/fpp/migration/publication/2013/10/asweetnessundodeathmifeeindonesiaenglish2.pdf)

Field research by FPP, Pusaka and Sawit Watch in 2013 collected residents' experiences in Baad, Wayau, Koa and Zanegi. Residents described incomplete information, promises of assistance and employment, imposed compensation terms and unclear agreements. Negotiations through selected leaders also fragmented collective customary decisions. Families depending on the land could bear the consequences without understanding the full terms of an agreement. [1](https://www.forestpeoples.org/fileadmin/uploads/fpp/migration/publication/2013/10/asweetnessundodeathmifeeindonesiaenglish2.pdf)

In Zanegi, land had been ceded to PT Selaras Inti Semesta, a Medco subsidiary, in 2009, before MIFEE's launch. Residents subsequently reported reduced hunting grounds and fish. Researchers recorded child malnutrition and illness, while paid employment had not replaced food previously obtained from forests and rivers. A doctor reportedly visiting for only one day each month illustrated the limited care available as health needs grew. [1](https://www.forestpeoples.org/fileadmin/uploads/fpp/migration/publication/2013/10/asweetnessundodeathmifeeindonesiaenglish2.pdf)

Losing forest access changed families' ability to obtain food and sustain life on their land. Promises of development confronted residents' experience of greater dependence on cash income, purchased food and company decisions. These village accounts explain the demands for protection of land rights and community consent in subsequent projects in Merauke. [1](https://www.forestpeoples.org/fileadmin/uploads/fpp/migration/publication/2013/10/asweetnessundodeathmifeeindonesiaenglish2.pdf)

The narrative must remain about those documented communities; company operations and later national programmes should be separately dated in the following accounts. No unsupported present-day village mortality figure is implied.
